Taleem360 pricing for schools

One price, published openly: $49 per month for up to 200 active student profiles, or 13,647 PKR per month for schools in Pakistan. A 30-day free pilot comes first, with no credit card. Larger schools, school groups and white-label deployments are quoted individually so the price tracks your enrolment rather than a bracket you happen to fall into.

The price

Licences are priced by the number of active student profiles, not by staff seats and not by module. A school with 200 students pays for 200 profiles and can create as many teacher and admin logins as it needs.

The published price is $49 per month, which covers up to 200 active student profiles and the full core suite listed below. Nothing in that list is a paid extra.

We invoice in your local currency. Schools in Pakistan are billed in PKR, the Gulf in AED or SAR, Bangladesh in BDT and Nigeria in NGN. The conversion below is indicative — the amount printed on your quote is the amount you pay.

What the price includes

The core suite is never metered and never sold back to you module by module. Everything below is included at the published price:

  • Attendance — biometric, QR/RFID and manual, with per-period registers
  • Fee collection — vouchers, partial payments, sibling discounts and arrears
  • Double-entry accounting — chart of accounts, journals, ledgers, trial balance
  • Examinations and configurable grading schemes
  • Report cards and marksheets
  • Parent portal — attendance, dues, results and announcements
  • Unlimited teacher and admin logins
  • WhatsApp and SMS parent notifications

What is quoted instead of published

Above the published profile limit, and for deployments that are not a single campus on a standard licence, we quote. This is deliberate: a school with 1,200 students and a school with 180 are not the same customer, and a published ladder would either overcharge one or undercharge the other.

  • More than 200 active student profiles — Larger schools are quoted per profile, so the price tracks actual enrolment rather than a bracket you happen to fall into.
  • Multi-campus and school groups — Central dashboards, per-campus isolation and cross-branch reporting are quoted per group.
  • White label — Your own brand, logo, colour scheme and domain on the platform — licensed and quoted per deployment.
  • Add-on modules — Daycare Hub, Skills Academy and Solo Pro are priced separately from the school ERP licence.

White label — run Taleem360 as your own platform

White label puts your brand on the entire platform: your logo, your colour scheme, your domain, and a branded parent portal that never mentions Taleem360. It is the right model for school chains, franchise networks and education groups that want a single branded system across every branch, and for academies and training providers that resell access to their own students.

White label is licensed and quoted per deployment, because the scope varies — a two-branch group and a national franchise network need very different things. Tell us your branch count, total student numbers and whether you need your own domain, and we will quote it.

Everything in the core suite comes with it. The difference is ownership of the surface, not the depth of the product.

  • Your logo, colour scheme and typography across every screen
  • Your own domain, including branded parent and staff subdomains
  • Parent portal and notifications sent under your name, not ours
  • Multi-campus central dashboard with per-branch isolation
  • Quoted per deployment — branch count and student numbers decide the price

The 30-day pilot

The pilot is a full deployment, not a demo. You get your own workspace, import your real student and staff data, and run live attendance and fee cycles for 30 days.

Nothing is charged during the pilot and no card is required. If you continue, your workspace and data carry over unchanged — there is no second migration.

What is not charged

There is no setup fee, no per-module charge, no charge for parent accounts, and no charge for onboarding support during the pilot. Annual and term-based billing are both available once you are on a paid licence.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Taleem360 cost?

The published price is $49 per month for up to 200 active student profiles, or 13,647 PKR per month for schools in Pakistan. A 30-day free pilot comes first and requires no credit card. Schools with more than 200 profiles, multi-campus groups and white-label deployments are quoted individually.

Do you charge per teacher or per student?

Per active student profile. Teacher and admin logins are unlimited at every licence level. The published price covers up to 200 profiles.

Can we pay in Pakistani rupees?

Yes. Schools in Pakistan are invoiced in PKR. The Gulf is invoiced in AED or SAR, Bangladesh in BDT and Nigeria in NGN. The conversion shown on this page is indicative; the amount on your quote is the amount you pay.

What happens if we have more than 200 students?

We quote you per profile. Larger schools are priced on their actual enrolment, so the price grows with the school rather than jumping at a bracket boundary. Send us your student count and campus count and we will quote it.

What is white label and who is it for?

White label puts your own brand, logo, colours and domain on the whole platform, including the parent portal. It suits school chains, franchise networks, education groups and academies that want one branded system across every branch, or that resell access to their own students. It is licensed and quoted per deployment.

What happens when the pilot ends?

Nothing is charged automatically. You choose a licence and your existing workspace continues with all data intact. If you do not continue, the workspace is deactivated.

Can we pay term by term instead of annually?

Yes. Term-based and annual billing are both available on paid licences.

Is there a discount for school groups?

Yes — groups running several campuses under one account are priced on consolidated profile counts. Contact us with your branch and student numbers for a quote.
