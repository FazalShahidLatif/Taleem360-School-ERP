Why schools in Karachi choose Taleem360

What Taleem360 does

Attendance that runs itself

Biometric and manual marking, per-period registers, staff duty rosters and automatic parent absence alerts over SMS and WhatsApp.

Fee collection without the queue

Vouchers, partial payments, sibling discounts, arrears tracking and reconciliation against a double-entry ledger — with mobile-wallet and bank channels.

Double-entry accounting built in

A real chart of accounts, journals, ledgers, trial balance and audit-ready financial statements. No spreadsheet exports required.

Report cards and exam workflows

Configurable grading schemes, term and board patterns, remark banks and one-click parent-visible report cards.

Parent portal and messaging

A branded parent login for attendance, dues, results and announcements, plus bulk messaging to any grade, section or route.

Multi-campus, multi-tenant

One account, many branches. Central dashboards with per-campus isolation, role-based access and white-labelled subdomains.
